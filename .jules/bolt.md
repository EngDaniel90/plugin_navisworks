## 2024-05-18 - Navisworks API COM Marshalling & Clash Overhead Bottlenecks
**Learning:** In Navisworks plugins, properties like `ComApiBridge.State` and `ComApiBridge.ToInwOpSelection` incur high O(N) marshalling overhead between .NET and COM boundaries when called inside a tight loop. Additionally, executing `TestsRunTest` iteratively during a descent simulation without an early exit (e.g., breaking out when a hard clash is detected) results in huge, unnecessary computational overhead, especially once a collision has already firmly occurred.
**Action:** Always extract and cache COM objects (like `InwOpState10` and `InwOpSelection`) before entering animation or simulation loops. Always implement early returns or breaks (`break;`) immediately upon confirming a valid clash result (`New` or `Active`) to skip redundant `TestsRunTest` evaluations in subsequent loop iterations.
