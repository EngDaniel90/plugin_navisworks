## 2024-05-29 - O(N) COM Marshalling Overhead in Navisworks Loops
**Learning:** Initializing COM wrappers (`ComApiBridge.State` and `ComApiBridge.ToInwOpSelection`) inside high-frequency simulation loops like `RunSimulation` creates massive and repetitive overhead by marshalling .NET objects to COM objects every frame or height step. Also, `TestsRunTest` is extremely expensive, and neglecting early exits causes redundant iterations.
**Action:** Always cache COM bridges (`InwOpState10`, `InwOpSelection`) outside of iteration loops and pass them to helper functions like `MoveItemsUsingCOM` or `ResetItemsUsingCOM`. Furthermore, immediately `break` from both the inner verification loop and the outer physics/simulation loop as soon as a target condition (like collision detection) is validated to skip expensive and redundant API calls.
