## 2024-05-18 - Navisworks COM API Caching and Early Exit in Simulations
**Learning:** In Navisworks plugins, `ComApiBridge.State` and `ComApiBridge.ToInwOpSelection` involve heavy marshalling. Creating them inside simulation loops (like moving items across multiple Z steps) causes significant overhead and garbage collection pressure. Furthermore, `TestsRunTest` is extremely expensive and must be exited immediately upon finding a valid collision to avoid redundant testing.
**Action:** Always cache `ComApi.InwOpState10` and `ComApi.InwOpSelection` outside of tight loops (e.g., in `RunSimulation` before the `while` loop) and pass them to helper methods like `MoveItemsUsingCOM`. Implement explicit early exit (`break`) strategies for both inner result evaluation loops and outer simulation loops as soon as `ClashResultStatus.New` or `Active` is detected.
