## 2024-05-24 - Navisworks Clash Simulation Loop Optimizations
**Learning:** In Navisworks API, repeatedly querying `doc.GetClash().TestsData` and marshalling .NET `ModelItemCollection` selections to COM (`ComApiBridge.ToInwOpSelection`) inside a simulation loop creates massive, redundant overhead (O(N) operations per frame). Additionally, executing `clashTestsData.TestsRunTest` is extremely computationally expensive and should be avoided once a collision is validated.
**Action:** Cache COM states (`ComApiBridge.State`) and COM selections (`ComApiBridge.ToInwOpSelection`) outside of iterative loops. Always implement immediate early exits (`break`) from both result-checking inner loops and simulation outer loops the moment a `ClashResultStatus.New` or `ClashResultStatus.Active` collision is detected.
